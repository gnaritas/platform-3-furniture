The building

An old station, a new errand

The store sits in Oakland’s former Grand Central Station building. The Mendozas purchased and renovated the property to open Platform 3 Furniture — giving an established commercial building new life and Oakland’s downtown another reason to visit.

“Purchasing the former Grand Central Station building and giving it a new life has been an exciting opportunity for us,” said Josie Mendoza at the store’s opening.

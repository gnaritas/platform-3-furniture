About · Oakland, Maryland · opened November 2026

From a station to a showroom

Platform 3 Furniture is the newest of several Garrett County businesses owned and operated by Raul and Josie Mendoza — and it lives in one of Oakland’s most recognized buildings, the former Grand Central Station.

The purchase

Giving an old building new life

The Mendozas — who already operate Don Patron Mexican Grill, Baja Street Wings and Tacos, and Baja Motel — purchased Oakland’s former Grand Central Station building and renovated it to house their latest venture, bringing new life to an established commercial building in the heart of downtown Oakland.

The store’s ribbon-cutting was hosted by the Garrett County Department of Business Development, with county commissioners, chamber of commerce leaders and local business representatives welcomed in attendance.

“With running two restaurants and a hotel, opening a furniture store wasn’t exactly on my bingo card. I’m so glad it was, though. This business has given us an increased ability to serve this community, and we are thankful for the support.”— Josie Mendoza, co-owner
“I want to say thank you to all the community partners who have made this possible. Everyone has been so gracious and helpful, and we cannot wait to meet your furniture needs.”— Raul Mendoza, co-owner

What the store is

A growing selection of furniture and mattresses, with new inventory arriving on an ongoing basis — a fresh, welcoming shopping experience for area residents.

Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: this site sets no cookies of its own, loads no advertising, and the only information it collects is what you type into the contact form.

The contact form

Your name, phone, email and message are sent to Platform 3 Furniture’s inbox so the shop can answer your question. They are used for that reply and nothing else — no lists, no marketing mail from this form.

The map

The pages that show the embedded map load it from Google Maps. Interacting with the map may set Google cookies under Google’s own privacy policy.

Hosting logs

The web host keeps standard request logs (page requested, time, browser type). They are used to keep the site running and are not sold or shared.

Questions

Ask through the contact form and a person will read it.
